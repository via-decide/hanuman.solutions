Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 21, 2026

Overview

Hanuman.Solutions provides engineering assessment, build, integration and production-rescue services. This policy explains the information handled through this website.

Information We Collect

  • Project intake: name, email, optional organisation, project description, current stage, engineering domain, and optional deadline and budget range.
  • Technical analytics: page, session and interaction events used to understand site operation and service interest.
  • Direct correspondence: information you choose to send by email.

How We Use Information

  • Review and respond to engineering enquiries.
  • Scope potential engagements and determine an appropriate technical entry point.
  • Operate, secure and improve the website and intake flow.
  • Meet legal obligations and prevent abuse.

Project Data Safety

Do not submit passwords, credentials, private keys, confidential source code or regulated personal data through project intake. We will arrange an appropriate secure exchange if an engagement requires sensitive technical material.

Service Providers and Sharing

We do not sell project-intake information. We use infrastructure and email-delivery providers to operate the site and deliver intake messages, and may disclose information when required by law.

Retention and Security

We retain enquiries only as long as reasonably required to respond, scope work, maintain business records and meet legal obligations. HTTPS and access controls protect data in transit and within the services used to deliver it, but no internet transmission is risk-free.

Your Choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of your project-intake information, subject to legal or operational record-keeping requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email dharam@viadecide.com.
