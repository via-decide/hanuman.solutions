Refund & Cancellation Policy

Hanuman Solutions is a production engineering and emergency system recovery service.

1. Diagnostic Fee Policy

Diagnostic fees are initiated once diagnostic work or active recovery efforts have commenced. Once analysis has begun, these fees are non-refundable as engineering resources are deployed immediately to trace the system failure.

2. System Recovery & Fixes

For custom debugging and system recovery agreements:

  • If a failure is determined to be non-recoverable or outside the specified recovery scope during our initial 12-hour audit, we will notify you immediately. Any pre-authorized billing for deployment fixes will be canceled.
  • If a solution is deployed and verified but subsequent unrelated system changes cause a separate failure, this will be handled as a separate incident intake.

3. Questions & Claims

If you have any billing queries, dispute claims, or wish to discuss an active service ticket, please contact us at: dharam@viadecide.com.
